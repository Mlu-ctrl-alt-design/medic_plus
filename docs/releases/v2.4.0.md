# v2.4.0 — Patient Portal

**Release date:** 2026-05-18

## What's new for patients

A new self-service portal at `https://<practice-host>/portal/<your-practice-slug>`:

- **Sign in with email only** — no password. A 6-digit code is emailed; expires in 10 minutes.
- **Book your own appointments** — pick a doctor, pick a date, pick a slot.
- **Cancel up to 24 hours before** — anything closer needs a phone call to reception.
- **See your records** — past visits, allergies, problems, chronic conditions (read-only, curated by your doctor).
- **Download your documents** — sick notes and prescriptions as PDFs.
- **View your invoices** — outstanding balances and PDF downloads. Online payment is deferred.
- **Update your contact details** — name, phone, email, address, self-reported allergies and medication.

## What's new for practices

- Patients can self-serve: appointments, records, documents, invoices, profile.
- The portal lives at `/portal/<your-slug>`. Each Patient record's `email` field is the sign-in identifier — make sure it's set.
- Mobile-first; works on a 360 px Android in a clinic waiting room.

## Behaviour to expect at upgrade

- The old Jinja `/portal` page is replaced by a React SPA. Bare `/portal` now:
  - Signed-out: prompts for a practice slug.
  - Signed-in with one Patient record: redirects to `/portal/<slug>`.
  - Signed-in with multiple: shows a practice picker.
- A patient signing in for the first time auto-provisions a User account with the `Patient` role. No invitations to send.
- A new `website_route_rule` maps `/portal/<slug>` to `/portal` with the slug in `form_dict`.

## Things this release does NOT do (deferred)

- Online payment of invoices.
- Patient self-reported allergies/conditions appearing in the doctor's structured allergy/condition lists (only the free-text fields are exposed).
- Multi-practice login under one session — sign out and back in if you switch practices.

## Technical notes

- New module: `medic_plus.api.patient_portal` (13 whitelisted endpoints).
- Shared booking helper `medic_plus.api.booking._book_slot` now used by both guest and authed flows.
- New PQCs registered/extended: `Patient Encounter` (Patient role branch), `Patient Problem List`, `Medication Request`, `Sales Invoice`.
- 18 Python tests + 5 Playwright tests added.
- Frontend bundle: `medic_plus/public/portal/` (10 JSX/JS/CSS files), loaded by `medic_plus/www/portal/index.html`.
- Reuses Meridian design system from `/daystar-health`; bridges `window.Icons` (Chevron/Calendar) into `window.MIcons` so the shared MSelect/MDatePicker render correctly.
